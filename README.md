### Matthieu Ostertag

I build latent-dynamics world models for industrial machines, and test whether they transfer to machines they were never trained on.

Engineering student at Mines Nancy (Université de Lorraine), joining École des Ponts (Institut Polytechnique de Paris) in 2027.

**Research**

- **World Models for Cross-Machine CNC Transfer under Partial Sensor Overlap**

  [paper](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.16071) · [code](https://github.com/ostertagmatthieu-dev/saac-jepa) · [project page](https://ostertagmatthieu-dev.github.io/saac-jepa/)
- Poster, BMVA Symposium on World Models (London, November 2026).
- Next: extending the model to multi-machine fleets and transferable decision policies.

**Community**

Industrial engineering at Mines Nancy, a year of mechanical engineering at Mannheim University of Applied Sciences, and strategy, data & AI work at Atos (France and UK).
